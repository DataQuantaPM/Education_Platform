Course Overview

Computer Science Fundamentals is designed for beginners who want to understand how computers work and develop strong problem-solving skills. This course follows Harvard CS50 and covers programming fundamentals, algorithms, memory, data structures, Python, and SQL through structured learning sessions.

Recommended for learners who are starting their computer science journey.
